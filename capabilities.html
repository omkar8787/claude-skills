Your global sourcing deskin India.

Five divisions that work alone or together, so you can go from an idea to a product in your customers' hands.

  • Division 01 · Find the product

    India Sourcing

    If it's made in India, we can help you find it.

    Describe what you need, and we investigate where and how India makes it. We look at clusters, artisans, export factories and GI producers, then bring back a feasibility view and a qualified shortlist.

    • Requirement analysis and written sourcing brief
    • Cluster and supplier-landscape research
    • Supplier identification and outreach
    • Document-based supplier verification
    • Comparison of quotes, MOQs and capabilities
    • GI and origin research for heritage products
    Explore India Sourcing Source a product

  • Division 02 · Make the product

    Manufacturing & Private Label

    Turn your product idea into a reality.

    OEM, ODM or private label: we help structure the specification, coordinate samples and packaging, and follow production until goods pass inspection.

    • Specification and tech-pack structuring
    • Sample-round management and feedback logs
    • Packaging and label specification
    • Production follow-up and status reporting
    • Golden-sample and approval management
    Explore Manufacturing & Private Label Let's build your product

  • Division 03 · Ship the product

    Global Export Solutions

    Made in India. Ready for the world.

    Export readiness, documentation coordination, HS-code research and destination-market compliance questions, organised before your goods leave the factory.

    • Export-readiness review
    • HS-code research (indicative; final classification is the importer's and broker's responsibility)
    • Destination compliance research
    • Commercial document review and checklists
    • Buyer–supplier communication
    Explore Global Export Solutions Check market readiness

  • Division 04 · Sell the product

    Brand Development & Advertising

    We don't just source products. We help build brands that sell.

    From positioning, naming and packaging to Amazon, Meta and Google advertising, we help turn products into market-ready brands, and keep growing them after launch.

    • Brand positioning and naming
    • Identity and packaging direction
    • Launch strategy
    • Amazon listing optimisation and A+ content strategy
    • Amazon PPC, Meta and Google campaign management
    • Shopify/D2C site planning and landing pages
    Explore Brand Development & Advertising Launch your brand with NGB

  • Division 05 · Procure with purpose

    Inclusive Procurement

    Business with purpose. Procurement with impact.

    NGB Ventures is a disability-owned business. Our Disability:IN DOBE® certification application is underway, and we work to open supply chains to smaller Indian producers.

    • Supplier-diversity engagement with corporate procurement teams
    • Sourcing that includes small producers and artisan enterprises
    • Transparent supplier documentation
    Explore Inclusive Procurement Connect with our inclusive procurement team

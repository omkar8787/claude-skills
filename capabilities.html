Our capabilities

Four divisions, one accountable partner.

India sourcing, OEM, ODM and private-label manufacturing, global export and brand development. Use one division or combine them, from product idea to your customer.
Request a Sourcing Proposal

  • Division 01 · Find the right maker

    India Sourcing & Supplier Discovery

    Find the right product and the right maker in India.

    Share your requirement and we research where and how India makes it: manufacturing clusters, export factories, artisan enterprises and GI producers. You receive a feasibility assessment and a qualified supplier shortlist.

    • Requirement analysis and a written sourcing brief
    • Manufacturing-cluster and supplier-landscape research
    • Supplier identification and outreach
    • Document-based supplier qualification
    • Like-for-like comparison of quotations, MOQs and capabilities
    • GI and origin research for heritage products
    Explore India Sourcing Request a sourcing proposal

  • Division 02 · Develop and produce

    OEM, ODM & Private Label Manufacturing

    Develop your product with India's manufacturers.

    Whether you need OEM production to your own design, an ODM product adapted from a manufacturer's range, or a private-label collection, we structure the specification, coordinate sampling and packaging, and follow production through to inspection.

    • Specification and tech-pack structuring
    • Sample-round management with written feedback
    • Packaging and label specification
    • Production follow-up and status reporting
    • Golden-sample approval and change control
    Explore OEM, ODM & Private Label Develop a private-label product

  • Division 03 · Ship with confidence

    Global Export Solutions

    Prepare every shipment for its destination market.

    Export readiness, export documentation, indicative HS-code research and destination-market compliance questions, resolved before goods leave the factory. Freight and customs clearance are handled by licensed partners.

    • Export-readiness review
    • Indicative HS-code research (final classification rests with the importer and customs broker)
    • Destination-market compliance research
    • Commercial document review and checklists
    • Communication between buyer and manufacturer
    Explore Global Export Discuss an export project

  • Division 04 · Build the brand

    Brand Development & Advertising

    Turn well-made products into brands that sell.

    Positioning, naming, identity and packaging, followed by marketplace listings and advertising on Amazon, Meta and Google. We help products made in India reach customers in your market.

    • Product positioning and naming
    • Brand identity and packaging direction
    • Launch planning
    • Amazon listing optimisation and A+ content
    • Amazon PPC, Meta and Google campaign management
    • Shopify and D2C site planning
    Explore Brand Development Build your global brand

How an enquiry works

  1. 01

    Share your requirement

    Describe the product, quantity, target price and destination market. Partial information is fine to start.

  2. 02

    Receive a feasibility assessment

    We assess whether, where and at what level India can make it, and flag compliance questions early.

  3. 03

    Review the proposed sourcing approach

    You receive a proposal covering manufacturing clusters, manufacturer types, sampling and timelines.

  4. 04

    Agree on the project scope

    Deliverables, responsibilities and fees are set out in a written agreement, with an NDA where needed.

  5. 05

    Begin supplier qualification and development

    We qualify manufacturers, manage samples and coordinate development towards an approved golden sample.

Overview

India → United Arab Emirates

The India–UAE Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) entered into force on 1 May 2022. The UAE, especially Dubai's Jebel Ali, is a major hub for re-export across the Gulf, Africa and beyond.

Sea routes from India's west-coast ports to the UAE are short, which makes the market attractive for replenishment programmes and mixed-product consolidation.

At a glance

Trade status
India–UAE CEPA has been in force since 1 May 2022. UAE customs duty under GCC rules is commonly 5% on CIF value for many goods, but check the specific HS line and any exemptions.
Offices
NGB operates from Pune, India. No local office.

Regulatory

Importing into United Arab Emirates

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

CEPA preference

Preferential duty needs goods that meet CEPA rules of origin, with a valid certificate of origin.

Conformity

Some product categories need conformity schemes such as ECAS or EQM via the UAE's standards authority.

Labelling

Arabic labelling may be needed for certain consumer goods.

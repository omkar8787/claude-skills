Overview

India → France

French fashion, home and beauty brands work with Indian embroidery, block print, textiles and natural ingredients.

France applies EU customs rules plus national requirements, such as the AGEC anti-waste law affecting labelling and the destruction of unsold goods.

At a glance

Trade status
France applies the EU Common Customs Tariff. Check EU–India FTA status before planning on preferential duty.
Offices
NGB operates from Pune, India. No local office.

Regulatory

Importing into France

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

AGEC law

Environmental product information and Triman/sorting labelling for many consumer products.

Cosmetics

EU 1223/2009 with a Responsible Person in the EU.

Textile EPR

France runs EPR schemes for textiles, footwear and household linen (Refashion).

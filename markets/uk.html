Overview

India → United Kingdom

British importers have long-standing links with Indian textiles, home décor and lifestyle products. UK retailers increasingly expect documented social compliance and sustainability evidence.

India and the UK signed a Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement in July 2025. Check its entry-into-force status and the product-specific rules of origin before relying on preferential duty.

At a glance

Trade status
The India–UK CETA was signed on 24 July 2025. Preferential tariffs only apply once the agreement is in force and the goods meet its rules of origin with valid proof of origin.
Offices
NGB operates from Pune, India. No local office.

Regulatory

Importing into the United Kingdom

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

UK Global Tariff

Check commodity codes and duty, plus preferential rates if the India–UK agreement applies to your goods and is in force.

Product safety

UKCA or CE marking where relevant, UK REACH, the GPSR-equivalent framework, and timber regulations for wood.

Hallmarking

Precious-metal items above exemption weights need UK hallmarking.

Claims

The CMA's Green Claims Code applies to environmental marketing.

FAQ

Importing from India into the United Kingdom: questions

Does the India–UK trade deal remove duty on my products?

It depends on your product's tariff line, the agreement's staging and whether it's in force when you import. Check the UK Trade Tariff and the rules of origin.

Occasions & planning

Plan around the calendar.

  • Diwali

    October–November. Plan by August for custom items.

  • Christmas & year-end

    December. Allow for peak courier and port congestion.

  • Eid & Ramadan

    Dates move each year with the lunar calendar.

  • Lunar New Year

    January–February. Ship early for Asian markets.

  • Onboarding & milestones

    Year-round. Keep a stocked kit for steady demand.

  • Conferences & retreats

    Fixed event dates. Build in buffer for customs.

Planning guide: allow roughly 8–12 weeks for custom-made or branded items, and more for large volumes or sea freight. We confirm the timeline for each programme.

How it works

From brief to doorstep.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Audience, number of recipients, budget per gift including delivery, delivery addresses, brand guidelines and dates.

  2. 02

    Curation

    Two or three concepts with item-level costs, lead times and any customs or food-rule flags.

  3. 03

    Sample box

    One physical sample box for approval. Changes are made here, not after production.

  4. 04

    Production & branding

    Items made or sourced, with engraving, printing, packaging and story cards.

  5. 05

    Assembly & checks

    Boxes packed and inspected, with photos shared before dispatch.

  6. 06

    Dispatch

    Bulk export to your warehouse or fulfilment partner, or multi-address courier delivery where practical.

Regulatory

Before you ship gifts abroad

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

Mixed food and non-food gifts

A single tea or spice item can bring the whole consignment under food import rules. Keep food in sealed, labelled retail packs and plan the import route accordingly.

Wood, plant and natural materials

Wooden items and packaging can need treatment or phytosanitary documents, and wood packaging follows ISPM 15. Some natural materials are restricted in certain markets.

Product safety

Food-contact items need migration testing, and candles, electronics and children's items carry their own rules. We check these at the concept stage.

Customs & low-value thresholds

Duty-free thresholds for low-value parcels have tightened in several markets, including the United States in 2025. We check current rules before planning multi-address delivery.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's the minimum order for corporate gifts?

Curated hampers from ready items can work from modest quantities. Custom-made or custom-branded items usually have higher minimums because of tooling and printing set-up. Share your numbers and we'll tell you what's practical.

Can we see a sample before ordering?

Yes. Every programme starts with an approved sample box. The cost of samples and courier depends on the items and is quoted upfront.

Can you add our logo?

Yes, through engraving, embossing, printing, embroidery or woven labels, depending on the material. Packaging and story cards can be fully branded.

Do you ship gifts directly to employees' homes abroad?

We can plan multi-address delivery where customs rules allow. Consolidating to a local fulfilment partner is often cheaper and more reliable, and we'll compare both.

Are you a gifting company with ready stock?

No. We curate and source to your brief from Indian makers and producers, so there is no catalogue of stock. That's what lets the programme feel specific to your brand.

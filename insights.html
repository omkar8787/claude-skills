How an enquiry works

  1. 01

    Share your requirement

    Describe the product, quantity, target price and destination market. Partial information is fine to start.

  2. 02

    Receive a feasibility assessment

    We assess whether, where and at what level India can make it, and flag compliance questions early.

  3. 03

    Review the proposed sourcing approach

    You receive a proposal covering manufacturing clusters, manufacturer types, sampling and timelines.

  4. 04

    Agree on the project scope

    Deliverables, responsibilities and fees are set out in a written agreement, with an NDA where needed.

  5. 05

    Begin supplier qualification and development

    We qualify manufacturers, manage samples and coordinate development towards an approved golden sample.

Cluster profile

Kannauj: attars and natural perfumery

Kannauj's perfumers use the traditional deg-bhapka copper-still method to make attars from flowers, herbs and earth (mitti attar).

Kannauj Perfume is a registered Geographical Indication (application 157).

Kannauj Perfume is a registered GI (application 157). GI registrations can be checked in the Geographical Indications Registry of India.

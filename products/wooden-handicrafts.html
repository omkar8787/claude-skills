Overview

Sourcing wooden handicrafts from India

From Saharanpur's carving tradition to Channapatna's lacquered toys, India's woodcraft clusters make décor, boxes, kitchen accessories and gifts.

Two things make wooden products export-ready: proper drying, which prevents cracking and mould, and timber-legality documentation, which matters increasingly for EU, UK and US buyers.

FAQ

Wooden Handicrafts: FAQs

What wood species are used in Indian handicrafts?

Mango wood, acacia and sheesham are common in export goods. Always declare the species, because legality rules and customs may require it.

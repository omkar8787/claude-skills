Overview

Sourcing cotton t-shirts from India

The cotton T-shirt is the archetypal Indian knitwear export. Tirupur's integrated supply chain covers yarn, knitting, dyeing, printing and garmenting, which makes it practical to specify the exact fabric your brand needs.

We help you turn a reference garment or tech pack into an approved sample, then follow bulk production through to inspection and shipment.

Compositions

Customisation

Options to specify

  • Fabric GSM, yarn count and compaction
  • Pantone colour matching via lab dips
  • Neck tape, labels and branding
  • Size spec and grading per market
  • Retail-ready barcoding

Quality assurance

What we check

  • Shrinkage and spirality testing
  • Colourfastness to washing
  • Measurement checks against the spec
  • AQL pre-shipment inspection

Minimum order quantities

Minimums depend on fabric. Stock-fabric programmes usually allow lower MOQs than custom-dyed or custom-knit fabric. We confirm actual MOQs with each manufacturer.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I order sample T-shirts before committing to bulk?

Yes. Sampling is a standard step: proto, then fit, then pre-production samples. Sample costs vary by manufacturer and fabric.

Can T-shirts be shipped directly to Amazon FBA?

Products can be labelled, polybagged and cartonised to Amazon's FBA requirements, and shipped through a freight forwarder that handles FBA deliveries. Check Amazon's current packaging and labelling rules before production.

Overview

Sourcing cotton bath towels from India

Terry towels are a major Indian home-textile export. Mills with integrated weaving, dyeing and finishing serve retail, hospitality and DTC brands.

Towel quality is measurable: GSM, absorbency, pile, shrinkage and colourfastness. We help you set the targets and test them.

FAQ

Cotton Bath Towels: FAQs

What GSM should a bath towel be?

It depends on how you're positioning it. Lighter towels dry faster, and heavier ones feel more plush. Order samples at two or three GSMs to decide.

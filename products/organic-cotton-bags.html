Overview

Sourcing organic cotton bags from India

Organic cotton bags (totes, drawstring pouches, produce bags) are a mainstay of retail, merchandising and DTC packaging. India produces organic cotton and has processing chains certified to GOTS and OCS.

The product is simple. The documentation isn't. We help you specify fabric weight, print and construction, and make sure certification covers the finished product.

At a glance

Fabric
Calico, twill, canvas (specify oz/GSM)
Certification
GOTS / OCS: verify scope certificates + transaction certificates
Printing
Screen print with water-based or certified inks
Handles
Self-fabric, webbing, rope

Minimum order quantities

Printed bag MOQs depend on fabric availability and print method. We confirm them per project.

Regulatory

Compliance considerations

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

Organic claims

Use "organic" only with valid certification across the supply chain and transaction certificates.

Green claims

Claims about environmental benefit must be substantiated, for example under the FTC Green Guides and UK CMA guidance.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a GOTS transaction certificate?

A transaction certificate (TC) is issued by the certifier for a specific shipment. It shows that the goods sold were certified. A scope certificate alone isn't enough to prove a particular order is organic.

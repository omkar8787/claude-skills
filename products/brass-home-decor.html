Overview

Sourcing brass home décor from India

Moradabad's metal-craft cluster makes brass décor for international retail: vases, bowls, candle holders, planters, sculptural objects and lighting.

The finish is the product. Antique, satin, polished, patinated or lacquered, it has to be developed, approved and held consistent across production.

FAQ

Brass Home Décor: FAQs

Will brass décor tarnish?

Brass naturally oxidises. Lacquer slows tarnishing, while unlacquered brass develops a patina. Decide which you want and say so in your product care information.

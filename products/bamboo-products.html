Overview

Sourcing bamboo products from India

India has significant bamboo resources, particularly in its north-eastern states, and a cane-and-bamboo craft tradition. Products include baskets, trays, lampshades, toothbrushes and homeware.

Bamboo needs treatment against insects and mould, and food-contact items need appropriate finishes. Bamboo-melamine composites face specific EU restrictions.

Regulatory

Compliance considerations

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

Food contact

Bamboo kitchenware must meet food-contact rules. The EU has acted against bamboo-filled plastic (melamine) items.

Phytosanitary

Untreated plant material may need phytosanitary certificates.

Bamboo Products: FAQs

Can bamboo toothbrushes be labelled compostable?

The handle may be, but bristles are usually nylon. Claims must reflect the whole product, or tell customers to separate the parts.

Overview

Sourcing natural loofah from India

Natural loofah is the dried fibrous skeleton of the sponge gourd (Luffa). It's a plant-based alternative to plastic bath and kitchen sponges, sold whole, cut into pads, or combined with cotton backing and straps.

Because it's an agricultural product, consistency (density, colour, size) and cleanliness matter. So does checking whether your market needs phytosanitary documentation.

FAQ

Natural Loofah: FAQs

Is natural loofah compostable?

Untreated loofah is a plant fibre. Backings, dyes and bleaching affect end-of-life claims, so substantiate any compostability claim.

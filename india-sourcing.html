What you can bring us

Almost any legitimate requirement.

A product you've seen

A photo, a link or a sample from another market. We find who in India can make it, or something better.

A specification

A tech pack, drawing or bill of materials. We find manufacturers with the right processes and certifications.

A category to explore

"Sustainable homeware" or "heritage textiles". We map clusters and options, then help you narrow down.

A heritage product

A GI or traditional craft. We research the region, the registered proprietors and the authorised users.

An honest promise: we can investigate almost anything India makes, but not every product can, or should, be sourced from India. Every request starts with a feasibility assessment covering capability, cost, compliance and volume. If the answer is no, we'll tell you.

Clear roles

What we do. What we coordinate.

We're explicit about who does what. NGB Ventures doesn't own factories, freight companies or labs. We work with independent, licensed partners, chosen per project.

How sourcing works

Seven steps, each with a deliverable.

  2. Output · Long-list

    We map the right clusters and manufacturer types, from artisan workshops to export factories, and approach a long-list.

  3. Output · Comparison matrix

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you have a list of verified suppliers I can browse?

No public directory. We match suppliers to each brief and verify them for that project. A static list can't tell you who's right for your product, volume and market.

Can you source from artisans and small producers?

Yes. Many of India's most distinctive products come from artisan clusters and small enterprises. We'll be clear about the realistic volumes, lead times and consistency for each.

How do you charge?

Fees are scoped per engagement, for example a fixed research fee, a project fee or a service fee. We agree it in writing before work starts. Ask us for a proposal.

What if I don't know the HS code or category?

That's fine. Describe the product and we'll work out the category, the likely clusters and the questions to answer.

How an enquiry works

  1. 01

    Share your requirement

    Describe the product, quantity, target price and destination market. Partial information is fine to start.

  2. 02

    Receive a feasibility assessment

    We assess whether, where and at what level India can make it, and flag compliance questions early.

  3. 03

    Review the proposed sourcing approach

    You receive a proposal covering manufacturing clusters, manufacturer types, sampling and timelines.

  4. 04

    Agree on the project scope

    Deliverables, responsibilities and fees are set out in a written agreement, with an NDA where needed.

  5. 05

    Begin supplier qualification and development

    We qualify manufacturers, manage samples and coordinate development towards an approved golden sample.

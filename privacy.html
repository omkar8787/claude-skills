Who we are

NGB Ventures ("NGB Ventures", "we") operates this website from Pune, Maharashtra, India. For privacy requests, email obarkade@gmail.com. [PLACEHOLDER: registered address.]

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we collect the details you provide: name, company, email, phone (optional), country, role and project information. We also record the page you submitted from. Our hosting provider may process standard server logs, such as IP address and user agent, for security.

Why we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, to prepare and deliver services you request, and to keep records of our correspondence. We don’t sell personal data.

Legal basis

We process enquiry data to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and for our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries. Where consent is required, we ask for it.

Sharing

With your knowledge, we may share project details (not your personal contact details unless necessary) with prospective manufacturers, inspection agencies or logistics partners, so we can deliver the services you request. Service providers that process data for us, such as email and hosting, act under contract.

International transfers

Data may be processed in India and in countries where our service providers operate. We take reasonable steps to protect it.

Retention

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to respond and for legitimate business record-keeping, then delete or anonymise them. [PLACEHOLDER: define the retention period.]

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data. Contact us to make a request.

Analytics

When enabled, we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC) to understand which pages are useful: pages viewed, approximate location, device type, and events such as submitting an enquiry or clicking an email or WhatsApp link. IP addresses are not stored by Google Analytics 4. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first, and analytics cookies are set only if they accept. Advertising features and ad personalisation are switched off. You can change your choice by clearing this site’s storage in your browser.

Email & WhatsApp enquiries

If the online form can’t deliver your enquiry, the site offers to open your email app or WhatsApp with your answers pre-filled. Nothing is sent until you press send in that app. Messages are then handled under that service’s own terms (for WhatsApp, Meta’s) and kept with our enquiry records.

Cookies & storage

This site doesn’t use advertising cookies. Your analytics choice, and the supplier checklist tool’s progress, are saved in your own browser’s local storage and aren’t sent to us. Google Analytics sets its own cookies only after consent where consent is required.

Before you ship

The questions that decide your landed cost.

  1. 01

    Classification

    Which HS code applies? It drives duty rates, product rules and paperwork. We research indicative codes. Final classification is the importer's and customs broker's decision.

  2. 02

    Origin & preference

    Does a trade agreement apply (e.g. India–UAE CEPA, India–Australia ECTA)? What proof of origin is needed? Preferences only apply when rules of origin are met.

  3. 03

    Product rules

    Safety, chemical, labelling and food-contact requirements in the destination market.

  4. 04

    Documents

    Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and product-specific certificates.

  5. 05

    Logistics

    Incoterm, mode (FCL, LCL or air), packing and insurance, coordinated with licensed forwarders.

What we never promise: guaranteed customs clearance, fixed duty rates or blanket tariff advantages. Tariffs and import requirements depend on HS classification, origin and destination, and they change, sometimes quickly.

FAQ

Export: FAQs

Are you a freight forwarder or customs broker?

No. We coordinate with licensed forwarders and customs brokers, who carry out carriage and clearance.

Can you tell me my exact import duty?

We can research indicative HS codes and point you to the official tariff tools. Exact duty is set by customs on the declared classification, origin and value. Confirm it with a licensed broker.

Do you handle Amazon FBA shipments?

We coordinate FBA-compliant labelling and cartons with the manufacturer, and hand over to forwarders that deliver to FBA warehouses.

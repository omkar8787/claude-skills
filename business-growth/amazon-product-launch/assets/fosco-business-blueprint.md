### 2.3 Durability Engineering

**Problem:** The #1 complaint across all competitors is crystals detaching.

**Solution — Three-Point Bonding System:**

1. **Wire Loop Capture:** Each stone sits inside a wire loop twisted 2× around it (mechanical hold)
2. **Micro-Dot Epoxy:** UV-cure adhesive applied at wire-stone contact (chemical bond)
3. **Tension Architecture:** Branches are pre-tensioned so wire naturally grips inward

**QC Protocol:** Each tree undergoes a 30-second inversion shake test before packaging. Any tree losing a stone is reworked.

## SECTION 8 — US COMPLIANCE

### 8.1 CPSIA (Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act)

**Applicability:** CPSIA primarily applies to children's products (intended for children 12 and under). If FOSCO is marketed as **adult home decor and spiritual wellness**, CPSIA testing is likely **not required**.

**Critical Rule:** Do NOT use words like "kids," "children," "toy," or "playroom" in any listing copy, packaging, or marketing. This would trigger children's product classification and require third-party lead testing ($500–$1,500).

### 8.2 California Proposition 65

**Risk Level: Medium.** Some crystals may contain trace minerals (lead, cadmium) that trigger Prop 65 thresholds.

**Action Required:**
1. Test representative samples at a CPSC-accredited lab for heavy metals ($200–$400)
2. If any substance exceeds Prop 65 thresholds, add a Prop 65 warning label
3. Standard Prop 65 label text: *"⚠ WARNING: This product can expose you to chemicals including [substance], which is known to the State of California to cause cancer/reproductive harm. For more information go to www.P65Warnings.ca.gov."*

**Cost of Non-Compliance:** Bounty hunter lawsuits in California ($2,500+ per violation). Budget for testing.

### 8.3 Required Product Labeling

| Label | Requirement | Placement |
|-------|------------|-----------|
| Country of Origin | "Made in India" | Product base + packaging |
| Material Composition | List stone types used | Packaging insert or box |
| Choking Hazard | Required ONLY if marketing to children | N/A if adult product |
| Prop 65 Warning | Required if testing shows threshold exceedance | Packaging |
| FTC Care Label | Not required for decorative stone products | N/A |

### 8.4 Amazon-Specific Compliance

- Amazon may request **SDS (Safety Data Sheets)** for stone/crystal products — have one prepared
- Ensure product is listed in correct category (Home Décor → Decorative Accessories, not Toys)
- Include accurate weight and dimensions to avoid FBA fee discrepancies

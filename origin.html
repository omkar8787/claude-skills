Verification & legal

How we label origin claims.

Registered GI

Registry details (application number, certificate, status) come from an indexed IP India GI registry record. We still recommend re-checking the live registry before commercial use.

Registered: renewal unconfirmed

The record's listed validity date has passed and we couldn't confirm a renewal.

Reported GI: unverified

We couldn't access the registry record. Details come from government or secondary sources and may be incomplete or conflicting.

Our role

NGB Ventures doesn't own any GI and isn't an authorised user. Products marketed with a GI name or logo must come from registered authorised users in the defined area. We help buyers find and verify them.

Primary register: IP India GI Registry (search.ipindia.gov.in). Research date for this collection: 3 Nov 2026.

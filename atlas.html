How to read the Atlas.

State profiles summarise widely documented regional manufacturing and craft specialisations. GI products link to records in our Origin Collection, each with its registry details, confidence level and sources.

The Atlas shows where capability is concentrated. It isn't a list of NGB suppliers or partners. Every sourcing request is researched and verified for that project.

The base map comes from the open-source @svg-maps/india dataset (CC BY 4.0). It shows boundaries as published in that dataset and may not reflect the latest administrative changes (for example, Ladakh as a separate Union Territory). It isn't to scale.

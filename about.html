Who we are

A structured route into India's manufacturing ecosystem.

NGB Ventures (Next Global Brand) is an India-based sourcing, manufacturing-coordination, export and brand-development business headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra.

India makes a very wide range of products, from GI-protected crafts to precision components. For international buyers, however, the supply base can be fragmented, difficult to verify and slow to navigate. NGB Ventures provides a single, accountable point of contact: we research product requirements, identify and qualify manufacturers, coordinate development and export, and help build the brand that sells the product.

NGB Ventures is a disability-owned business and exports under its own Importer-Exporter Code (IEC). Disability:IN DOBE® certification application in progress.

How we work

Four principles

01

Feasibility first

Every enquiry starts with an honest assessment. If India, a particular cluster or a target price is not right for your product, we say so early.

02

Evidence over claims

Registrations, certificates and GI status are checked against their sources. Anything we cannot verify is labelled as unverified.

03

Clear roles

We state what we deliver ourselves and what we coordinate through independent, licensed partners, from the factory to the port.

04

Access for smaller makers

Many of India's best products come from small producers. We work to bring them within reach of international buyers.

Confidential by default

Confidential by default. Clear terms for everyone.

We work under NDA and do not display client names or work. Every engagement is set out in a written agreement.

  • We sign an NDA

    We sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share designs, specifications, pricing or launch plans. Project details are shared only with the manufacturers and partners who need them.

  • No client names on display

    We do not publish client names, logos or products. That is why this site shows no client brands.

  • Clear terms for everyone

    Project confidentiality, defined responsibilities and written commercial agreements form part of our engagement process, so the arrangement works for buyers, makers and partners alike.

  • Focused on India

    We source exclusively from manufacturers, producers and artisan enterprises in India.

Supplier diversity

Import from a disability-owned exporter.

NGB Ventures is a disability-owned business and exports under its own IEC. When you import through us, you buy directly from the business that has applied for DOBE® certification.

DOBE® certification

Disability:IN DOBE® certification application in progress. The points below describe what certification could enable if granted. We do not display a certified-supplier badge.

Certification does not guarantee contracts, preferred status or pricing. Benefits depend on your supplier-diversity programme and its eligibility rules; please confirm with your supplier-diversity team how an India-based, disability-owned exporter is recognised.

How an enquiry works

  1. 01

    Share your requirement

    Describe the product, quantity, target price and destination market. Partial information is fine to start.

  2. 02

    Receive a feasibility assessment

    We assess whether, where and at what level India can make it, and flag compliance questions early.

  3. 03

    Review the proposed sourcing approach

    You receive a proposal covering manufacturing clusters, manufacturer types, sampling and timelines.

  4. 04

    Agree on the project scope

    Deliverables, responsibilities and fees are set out in a written agreement, with an NDA where needed.

  5. 05

    Begin supplier qualification and development

    We qualify manufacturers, manage samples and coordinate development towards an approved golden sample.

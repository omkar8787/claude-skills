Who we are

A modern gateway to India's manufacturing ecosystem.

NGB Ventures, for Next Global Brand, is an India-based sourcing, manufacturing-coordination, export and brand-development business headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra.

We started from a simple observation. India makes an extraordinary range of products, from GI-protected crafts to precision components, but for international buyers it can feel fragmented, hard to verify and slow to navigate. We want to be the single point of access: one desk that can investigate almost any legitimate product requirement, find the right makers, develop and export the product, and help build the brand that sells it.

NGB Ventures is a disability-owned business. Our Disability:IN DOBE® certification application is underway, and every shipment we export goes out under our own IEC, so buyers import directly from a disability-owned business. It's part of the business, not a footnote.

How we work

Four principles

01

Feasibility first

Every request starts with an honest assessment. If India, a cluster or a target price isn't right for your product, we say so early.

02

Evidence over claims

Registrations, certificates and GI status are checked against their sources. What we can't verify, we label as unverified.

03

Clear roles

We state what we deliver and what we coordinate through independent, licensed partners. No grey areas at the factory or the port.

04

Access for more makers

India's best products often come from small producers. We work to bring them within reach of global buyers.

Confidential by default

Your brand stays yours. Win-win, by design.

We work under NDA, so we never put client names or work on show. And every engagement is built to work for everyone involved.

  • We sign an NDA

    Before you share designs, specifications, pricing or plans, we sign a non-disclosure agreement. Your brief stays between us and the makers who need it.

  • No client names on display

    We never show client names, logos or products. That's why you won't see brand logos anywhere on this site. Our work stays yours.

  • Win-win, by design

    We structure every engagement so it works for everyone involved: you, the makers and every partner along the way. Your interests are protected, always.

  • Made in India, every time

    Everything we source, make and ship comes from India's own makers, farms and factories.

How we measure

Numbers that decide growth.

ROAS
Return on ad spend: revenue attributed to ads ÷ ad spend. Useful, but it can flatter retargeting.
CAC
Customer acquisition cost: total acquisition spend ÷ new customers. It decides whether growth is profitable.
CVR
Conversion rate: orders ÷ sessions. Listing and landing-page quality move it the most.
TACoS
Total advertising cost of sales on Amazon: ad spend ÷ total revenue. It shows organic lift over time.

We report every month in plain language. We don't publish client results we don't have, and we don't promise outcomes.

FAQ

Advertising: FAQs

What does a typical engagement include?

Usually an audit or launch plan, account setup or restructure, creative and listing work, campaign management and monthly reporting. Scope and fees are agreed per brand.

Do you work with brands outside India?

Yes. Most of our advertising focus is international marketplaces and D2C sites in the US, UK, EU, UAE and Australia.

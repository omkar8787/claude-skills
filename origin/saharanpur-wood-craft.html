Characteristics

  • Intricate hand carving in hardwoods such as sheesham
  • Floral and geometric relief, lattice and inlay
  • Furniture, boxes, screens and décor

Cultural history

Saharanpur's carving tradition developed over generations of craftsmen and is now a major woodcraft export centre.

Compliance considerations

Points to raise with your compliance advisor, lab or customs broker. This isn't legal advice.

  • Timber legality: EUDR (check dates), US Lacey Act, UKTR
  • ISPM-15 for wood packaging
  • Moisture content and finish testing
  • GI names and logos are protected under India's Geographical Indications of Goods Act, 1999. Use them only for goods from registered authorised users in the defined area. NGB Ventures doesn't own this GI and isn't an authorised user.

Characteristics

  • Crisp savoury snack made from moth bean (and gram) flour
  • Seasoned with spices and edible oil
  • Characteristic texture linked to local ingredients and arid climate

Cultural history

Bhujia-making in Bikaner dates to the late 19th century and grew into one of India's best-known snack industries.

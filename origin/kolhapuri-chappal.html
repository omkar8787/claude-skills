Characteristics

  • Hand-crafted vegetable-tanned leather sandals
  • Hand-braided straps and toe loop; no nails in traditional construction
  • Regional patterns such as Kapshi and Paytan

Cultural history

Made by leather-working communities for centuries, Kolhapuri chappals drew global attention in 2025 when a luxury fashion house showed a similar design, which prompted debate about GI protection and attribution.

Authorised users

Over 900 authorised users have been reported (secondary source). Verify on the register.

Public producer information

Artisan leather workers and small units across the defined districts, supported by LIDCOM and LIDKAR.

Export potential

Fashion and DTC footwear with heritage storytelling. Collaborations need proper GI attribution.

Potential target countries

Authentication & traceability

Source from registered authorised users, and request LIDCOM/LIDKAR documentation.

Compliance considerations

Points to raise with your compliance advisor, lab or customs broker. This isn't legal advice.

  • REACH chromium VI limits for leather in skin contact (EU/UK)
  • Footwear material labelling (EU Directive 94/11/EC)
  • Use of the "Kolhapuri" name is protected for GI-compliant products
  • GI names and logos are protected under India's Geographical Indications of Goods Act, 1999. Use them only for goods from registered authorised users in the defined area. NGB Ventures doesn't own this GI and isn't an authorised user.

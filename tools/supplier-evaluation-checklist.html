Supplier evaluation checklist

A structured way to assess an Indian supplier before you commit. Progress is saved in your browser.

01Business verification

0/6

  • GSTIN verified on the GST portal and matches legal name

  • Importer-Exporter Code (IEC) verified with DGFT

  • Company/LLP registration checked (MCA) or proprietorship documents seen

  • Confirmed manufacturer vs. trading company

  • Factory address verified (visit, video call or third-party check)

  • Bank account name matches the business entity

02Factory capability

0/4

  • Machinery list matches the product's processes

  • Key processes in-house (or subcontractors disclosed)

  • Has produced similar products (evidence seen)

  • Sampling / development capability

03Production capacity

0/5

  • Monthly capacity stated and plausible

  • Current utilisation and order book discussed

  • Lead times confirmed for sampling and bulk

  • Seasonal or peak constraints understood

  • Subcontracting policy agreed in writing

04Quality management

0/5

  • Dedicated QC staff

  • Inline inspection process in place

  • Agrees to AQL standard and third-party inspection

  • Testing equipment or lab partnerships

  • Corrective-action process for defects

05Certifications

0/4

  • Certificates verified in the issuing body's database

  • Certificate scope covers this product and site

  • Social-compliance audit available (if required)

  • Product certifications or test reports available (if required)

06Export experience

0/4

  • Exports to your region (evidence seen)

  • Familiar with your required export documents

  • Understands the agreed Incoterm

  • References from existing export buyers

07Sample evaluation

0/6

  • Sample matches the approved specification

  • Measurements within tolerance

  • Materials and components as specified

  • Workmanship and finish acceptable

  • Packaging and labelling as specified

  • Sample delivered on the agreed timeline

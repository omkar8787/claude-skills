    Find the product and the maker

    We map India's clusters, producers and GI origins against your requirement, then build a qualified shortlist.

  2. 02 · Manufacture

    Make it to specification

    Samples, golden-sample approval, production follow-up and inspections, coordinated with independent manufacturers.

  3. 03 · Customize

    Make it yours

    Materials, finishes, sizes, private-label branding and packaging, specified and approved before bulk production.

    Give it a name worth remembering

    Positioning, identity and packaging that make the product make sense to your customers.

  5. 05 · Advertise

    Put it in front of buyers

    Amazon, Meta and Google campaigns, influencer seeding and PR, measured on ROAS and CAC.

  6. 06 · Export

    Ship it to the world

    Documentation, compliance questions and logistics, coordinated with licensed forwarders and brokers.

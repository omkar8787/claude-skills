How a project moves

Source. Manufacture. Customise. Brand. Advertise. Export.

  1. 01 · Source

    Find the product and the maker

    We map India's manufacturing clusters, producers and GI origins against your requirement, then build a qualified shortlist.

  2. 02 · Manufacture

    Make it to specification

    Samples, golden-sample approval, production follow-up and inspections, coordinated with independent manufacturers.

  3. 03 · Customise

    Make it yours

    Materials, finishes, sizes, private-label branding and packaging, specified and approved before bulk production.

  4. 04 · Brand

    Give it a name worth remembering

    Positioning, identity and packaging that make the product make sense to your customers.

  5. 05 · Advertise

    Put it in front of buyers

    Amazon, Meta and Google campaigns, creator partnerships and PR, measured on return on ad spend and acquisition cost.

  6. 06 · Export

    Ship it to the world

    Documentation, compliance questions and logistics, coordinated with licensed forwarders and brokers.

Supplier diversity

Import from a disability-owned exporter.

NGB Ventures is a disability-owned business and exports under its own IEC. When you import through us, you buy directly from the business that has applied for DOBE® certification.

DOBE® certification

Disability:IN DOBE® certification application in progress. The points below describe what certification could enable if granted. We do not display a certified-supplier badge.

  1. 01

    Buy directly from the disability-owned business

    NGB Ventures exports under its own Importer-Exporter Code (IEC) as exporter of record, so your purchase order and invoice are with the disability-owned business itself.

  2. 02

    Supplier-diversity reporting

    If certification is granted, purchases may be reportable as disability-owned spend, depending on your supplier-diversity programme and its eligibility rules.

  3. 03

    Tier 2 reporting for your customers

    If you supply large retailers or corporations, purchases from a certified diverse supplier may support their Tier 2 reporting, subject to their programme rules.

  4. 04

    Independent ownership verification

    DOBE® certification involves Disability:IN verifying that a business is at least 51% owned, managed and controlled by people with disabilities.

  5. 05

    Visibility to procurement teams

    Certified businesses can be found through Disability:IN's supplier resources used by corporate procurement teams.

  6. 06

    The same commercial standards

    Supplier diversity does not replace commercial rigour: NDAs, supplier qualification, independent inspection and written agreements remain standard.

Certification does not guarantee contracts, preferred status or pricing. Benefits depend on your supplier-diversity programme and its eligibility rules; please confirm with your supplier-diversity team how an India-based, disability-owned exporter is recognised.

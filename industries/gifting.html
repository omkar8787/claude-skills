Overview

Curated, branded gift programmes built from Indian craft, textiles, tea, spices and design, assembled and dispatched for global teams.

The best corporate gifts are things people keep. India's craft clusters and food producers offer an unusual depth of gift material: hammered brass from Moradabad, block prints from Jaipur, single-estate teas, spice collections, handloom textiles, carved wood and natural fragrance. Each comes with a story your recipients can read.

NGB Ventures turns those materials into a gift programme. We curate to your budget and brand, develop custom items where needed, manage branding and packaging, assemble the boxes, and plan dispatch to one warehouse or to many addresses. Every element is sampled before production.

Regulatory

Compliance considerations

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

Mixed food and non-food gifts

A single tea or spice item can bring the whole consignment under food import rules. Keep food in sealed, labelled retail packs and plan the import route accordingly.

Wood, plant and natural materials

Wooden items and packaging can need treatment or phytosanitary documents, and wood packaging follows ISPM 15. Some natural materials are restricted in certain markets.

Product safety

Food-contact items need migration testing, and candles, electronics and children's items carry their own rules. We check these at the concept stage.

Customs & low-value thresholds

Duty-free thresholds for low-value parcels have tightened in several markets, including the United States in 2025. We check current rules before planning multi-address delivery.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What budget do I need for corporate gifts from India?

Gift programmes work across a wide range of budgets. Tell us your per-gift budget including delivery, and we'll show what fits. Custom-made items and multi-address delivery cost more than bulk shipments to one address.

How far ahead should we plan festive gifting?

As a planning guide, allow 8–12 weeks for custom-made or branded items, and longer for large volumes or sea freight. Curated sets from ready items can move faster. We confirm timing for each project.

Can you ship to employees in different countries?

We can plan multi-address delivery with courier partners, subject to each destination's customs rules. For many recipients in one country, shipping in bulk to a local fulfilment partner is often cheaper.

Can we include Indian sweets or snacks?

Shelf-stable, sealed and properly labelled products are possible for some markets. Fresh sweets usually aren't practical for export. We'll advise per destination.

Overview

Sports balls, cricket gear, fitness accessories and activewear.

Jalandhar in Punjab and Meerut in Uttar Pradesh are India's best-known sports-goods centres. Their makers produce cricket bats and balls, inflatable balls, protective gear and fitness accessories. India's textile clusters add activewear and yoga apparel.

Sports goods have performance specifications: weight, size, rebound, durability and safety. Testing against the right standard is part of the product brief.

Regulatory

Compliance considerations

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

Product safety

General product-safety rules apply: GPSR in the EU, GPSR-equivalent law in the UK, CPSC in the US. Some products have specific EN or ASTM standards.

Chemicals

Children's items

FAQ

Sports & Fitness: buyer FAQs

Where are cricket bats made in India?

Jalandhar and Meerut are the best-known centres. Kashmir willow bats are also made in Jammu & Kashmir.

Can I private-label yoga mats from India?

Yes, depending on the material (cotton, jute, natural rubber, TPE, PVC). Material choice affects compliance and sustainability claims.

Overview

Metal, wood, enamel, stoneware and serveware for retail and hospitality.

India supplies kitchenware and tableware in stainless steel, copper, brass, enamel, wood, stoneware and glass. Moradabad's metalworking base and wood-turning workshops are particularly relevant for serveware and hospitality buyers.

Food contact is non-negotiable. Material grades, coatings, linings and testing decide whether a beautiful piece can legally hold food in your market.

Regulatory

Compliance considerations

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

EU/Germany

Framework Regulation (EC) 1935/2004. Many German retailers expect LFGB-based testing.

United States

FDA food-contact requirements, plus California Prop 65 for lead and other substances.

Reactive metals

Copper and brass that touch food usually need a lining, or labelling as decorative only.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are copper mugs safe for food and drink?

Unlined copper reacts with acidic foods. Many buyers specify stainless or tin-lined interiors, plus food-contact testing for the destination market.

What testing do I need for wooden serving boards?

Food-contact compliance depends on the finish and the market. Use food-safe oils and test to the market's requirements.

Overview

Paper, board, rigid boxes, jute, cotton and eco-packaging for retail and e-commerce.

Packaging can be a stand-alone sourcing category, or the final stage of every product we coordinate. India's packaging supply base includes printing hubs such as Sivakasi in Tamil Nadu, known for printing, plus corrugated, rigid-box and textile-packaging makers across the country.

Good packaging is engineered. It has to protect the product, fit the carton and the container, meet retailer and e-commerce rules, and present your brand. We help develop dielines, materials and print specs alongside product development.

Regulatory

Compliance considerations

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

EU packaging rules

The EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) introduces recyclability and labelling requirements in phases. Check the application dates.

Food contact

Food-contact packaging must meet EU 1935/2004, FDA 21 CFR or other market rules.

EPR

Extended producer responsibility schemes in many markets require the importer or brand to register and report packaging.

Certified claims

FSC or recycled-content claims need valid chain-of-custody certification.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can packaging be sourced together with my product?

Yes, and that's often the most efficient route. We coordinate packaging development with the product manufacturer, so packing happens at the source and goods arrive retail-ready.

What MOQs apply to custom printed boxes?

Offset printing usually needs higher minimums than digital printing. We'll compare options for your quantities.

Do you offer plastic-free packaging?

We can source paper, board, moulded-pulp, jute and cotton alternatives. Performance needs testing, because plastic-free formats protect products differently.

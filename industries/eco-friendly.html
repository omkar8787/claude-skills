Overview

Jute, bamboo, coir, organic cotton, loofah and low-plastic alternatives.

India is a major producer of natural fibres. It's among the world's largest jute producers, and Kerala's Alappuzha region is closely associated with coir. Bamboo, organic cotton, loofah and other plant materials give international retailers and DTC brands credible alternatives to plastic.

Regulators now scrutinise sustainability claims. The EU's rules against greenwashing, the UK CMA's Green Claims Code and the US FTC Green Guides all expect claims to be specific and evidenced. We help buyers source eco products along with the documents that support their claims.

Regulatory

Compliance considerations

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

Green claims

Claims such as "biodegradable" or "eco-friendly" need substantiation under the FTC Green Guides, the UK CMA Green Claims Code and EU consumer law.

Phytosanitary

Some natural products such as coir, plant materials and loofah may need phytosanitary certificates or fumigation for specific markets.

Food contact

Bamboo and leaf tableware must meet food-contact rules. Bamboo-melamine composites face specific EU restrictions.

Certified organic

"Organic" textile claims usually need GOTS or OCS certification with transaction certificates.

FAQ

Eco-Friendly Products: buyer FAQs

Can I call jute bags biodegradable?

Untreated jute is a natural fibre. But laminations, dyes, prints and handles change the picture. Claims must reflect the finished product and be substantiated. We'll flag which parts affect a claim.

What documents prove organic cotton?

GOTS or OCS scope certificates for each processing site, plus transaction certificates (TCs) for your specific shipment.

Do natural products need fumigation?

Some markets need phytosanitary certification or treatment for plant-based goods. Requirements vary by product and destination, so check before shipping.

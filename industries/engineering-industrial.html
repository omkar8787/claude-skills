Overview

Castings, forgings, machined components, fasteners, auto parts and fabrications.

India's engineering base is deep and regionally specialised. Pune is one of India's major automotive and engineering hubs. Rajkot is known for castings, diesel engines, pumps and machine-tool components. Ludhiana is known for hand tools, bicycle parts and fasteners.

Engineering sourcing runs on drawings, material specs, tolerances and documents. We help structure RFQs, check drawing feasibility with manufacturers, coordinate first-article inspection and manage documentation such as material test certificates.

Regulatory

Compliance considerations

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

CE / UKCA

Machinery and certain products need conformity assessment for EU or UK sale.

Material traceability

Mill certificates and heat-number traceability for critical parts.

Trade remedies

Some steel and engineering products face anti-dumping or safeguard duties in certain markets. Check before ordering.

FAQ

Engineering & Industrial Products: buyer FAQs

Can you source build-to-print components from my drawings?

Yes. We share drawings under NDA with suitable manufacturers, gather feasibility feedback and quotations, and coordinate samples and first-article inspection.

What documents should accompany machined parts?

Usually dimensional inspection reports, material test certificates, heat-treatment certificates (if relevant) and packing lists. For automotive, PPAP-style submissions.

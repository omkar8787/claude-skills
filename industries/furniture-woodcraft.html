Overview

Solid-wood, iron and mixed-material furniture for retail, hospitality and DTC.

Indian furniture exports are concentrated in a few strong hubs. Jodhpur, in Rajasthan, is widely known for solid-wood and iron-and-wood furniture. Saharanpur is known for carved woodwork. Common export species include mango wood, acacia and sheesham (Indian rosewood).

Furniture is an engineering problem as much as a design one. Moisture content, joinery, finishing, knock-down construction and packaging all decide whether a product arrives intact and survives use. We coordinate those details with manufacturers before bulk production starts.

Manufacturing capabilities

What Indian manufacturers commonly offer

01

Kiln drying

Controlled kiln drying to a target moisture content suited to the destination climate is critical. Specify it.

02

Knock-down design

Converting assembled designs to KD or flat-pack to cut freight cost. Needs engineering and test assembly.

03

Mixed materials

Wood combined with powder-coated iron, cane, stone tops or upholstery.

04

Finishing systems

Water-based and PU lacquers, waxes and oils. Finish choice affects compliance and durability.

Regulatory

Compliance considerations

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

Timber legality

EUDR due diligence (application dates have shifted, so check the current timeline), US Lacey Act and UKTR for wood origin.

Formaldehyde

Composite wood components for the US must comply with TSCA Title VI. Similar limits apply in the EU.

Flammability (upholstered)

Upholstered furniture may fall under UK Furniture and Furnishings (Fire) (Safety) Regulations or California TB117-2013.

ISPM-15

Wooden crates and pallets need ISPM-15 heat treatment and marks.

FAQ

Furniture & Woodcraft: buyer FAQs

Is solid mango wood furniture durable?

Mango wood is a widely used, fairly dense hardwood in Indian export furniture. Its durability depends heavily on correct kiln drying and finishing. We recommend specifying target moisture content and checking it during inspection.

Can furniture be shipped flat-pack from India?

Many designs can be converted to knock-down construction. It usually needs design changes to joints, a pre-production test assembly, and clear instructions.

What should a furniture inspection cover?

Dimensions, construction, moisture content, finish, function (drawers, doors), stability, packaging and shipping marks. A golden sample is the reference point.

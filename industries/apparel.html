Overview

Knitwear, wovens, basics and private-label collections from India's established garment clusters.

India is one of the few sourcing countries that can cover the whole cotton chain inside its own borders: fibre, spinning, knitting or weaving, dyeing, printing, garmenting and finishing. For buyers, that means shorter material pipelines and more control over fabric development.

Different clusters do different things. Tirupur is synonymous with cotton knitwear. Ludhiana is known for hosiery and woollen knits. Jaipur blends block-printed fabrics with fashion garmenting. NGB Ventures helps you match your product, quantity and compliance needs to the right type of manufacturer, then coordinates sampling, production follow-up, inspection and shipment.

Manufacturing capabilities

What Indian manufacturers commonly offer

01

Vertical cotton supply

Many mills and garment units sit close together, so it's often practical to develop custom fabric (GSM, blend, finish) instead of buying stock.

02

Knit and woven expertise

Circular knitting dominates in knitwear clusters. Woven fashion and shirting draw on mills in other regions.

03

Print and surface

Screen, digital and traditional hand block printing, embroidery, garment dyeing and washes.

04

Sustainable inputs

Organic and recycled cotton blends are widely offered. Ask for certification at every processing stage, not just a fibre claim.

Regulatory

Compliance considerations

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

Fibre & care labelling

Your market sets the label rules. The US has the FTC Textile Act and Care Labeling Rule. The EU uses Regulation (EU) 1007/2011 for fibre names.

Chemical restrictions

EU REACH restricted substances, California Proposition 65, and your own brand RSL. Third-party lab testing is recommended.

Children's products

Children's apparel for the US is covered by CPSIA, including tracking labels and lead and phthalate limits where relevant. Drawstring rules also apply.

Voluntary certifications

GOTS, OCS, GRS and OEKO-TEX are supplier-level certifications. Check the certificate scope and validity in the certifier's database.

FAQ

Apparel & Garments: buyer FAQs

What MOQs are typical for apparel manufacturing in India?

It depends on the fabric. When stock fabric is available, many units will consider a few hundred pieces per style. Custom-dyed or custom-knit fabric usually needs larger runs per colour. We confirm the real MOQ with each manufacturer before you commit.

Can you develop a private-label T-shirt line from a tech pack?

Yes. We can coordinate tech-pack review, fabric sourcing, sampling rounds, label and packaging development, and bulk production follow-up. If you don't have a tech pack yet, we can work from a reference garment.

Which certifications should I ask garment suppliers for?

That depends on your claims and market. To make organic claims, you need GOTS or OCS certification across the chain. Social-compliance audits such as SMETA or amfori BSCI are often requested by retailers. Always check the certificates in the issuing body's public database.

How long does apparel sampling take?

It varies with fabric availability and complexity. Proto samples from stock fabric can be quick. Custom fabric development adds lab-dip and knitting or weaving time. We share a sample timeline once we've qualified suppliers.

Overview

Natural cosmetics, hair care, soaps, Ayurvedic-inspired formulations and private label.

India has a large contract-manufacturing base for personal care. It ranges from handmade cold-process soaps to GMP facilities producing creams, oils, shampoos and serums. Ayurvedic and botanical ingredients give brands a natural point of difference.

Beauty is one of the most regulated categories we coordinate. The formula, the claims and the label have to work for your destination market before production, not after. We help structure that process with manufacturers and your regulatory advisors.

Regulatory

Compliance considerations

These are considerations to raise with your compliance advisor, testing lab or customs broker. They aren't legal advice. Regulations change, so verify current requirements for your product and market.

EU & UK

Regulation (EC) 1223/2009 and UK cosmetics rules: Responsible Person, Product Information File, safety assessment, CPNP or SCPN notification.

United States

MoCRA (2022) adds facility registration, product listing, adverse-event reporting and safety substantiation.

Claims

Therapeutic or Ayurvedic medicinal claims can reclassify a product. Keep cosmetic claims within the rules.

Animal testing

The EU and UK ban animal testing for cosmetics. Get supplier declarations.

FAQ

Beauty & Personal Care: buyer FAQs

Can I launch a private-label skincare brand using an Indian manufacturer?

Yes. Many contract manufacturers offer stock formulations you can customise. Budget for destination-market requirements such as an EU Responsible Person and safety assessment, or MoCRA listing in the US.

Is ISO 22716 required?

It isn't universally mandatory, but it's the recognised cosmetic GMP guideline and is referenced under EU law. Many retailers expect it. Check that the certificate is valid and covers the site.

Can 'Ayurvedic' be used on labels abroad?

Descriptive use may be possible, but medicinal claims usually aren't allowed for cosmetics. Have your claims reviewed for each market.

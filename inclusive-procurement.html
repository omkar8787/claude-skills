What DOBE® means

A recognised diverse-supplier category.

The Disability-Owned Business Enterprise (DOBE®) certification is run by Disability:IN. Its published criteria require, among other things, that a business is for-profit and at least 51% owned, operated, managed and controlled by one or more people with disabilities. It must also be headquartered in the country where it applies.

Many large companies run supplier-diversity programmes that track spend with certified diverse suppliers, including disability-owned businesses. Certification can open conversations with those procurement teams.

Certification and supplier-diversity programmes may support procurement engagement. They don't guarantee contracts, funding or preferred purchasing, and we never suggest otherwise.

FAQ

Inclusive procurement: FAQs

Is NGB Ventures DOBE® certified?

Not yet. Our application/process is underway. We'll update this page and our materials only after Disability:IN formally grants certification.

Can certification guarantee us a contract?

No. Supplier-diversity programmes vary by company. Certification may help start a conversation, but contracts are earned on capability, price and service.

Do you work only with disability-owned suppliers?

No. We source from a broad range of Indian manufacturers and producers. Inclusion means widening access, not narrowing choice.

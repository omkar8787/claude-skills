FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share sensitive information such as designs, specifications, pricing or launch plans.

Why don't you show client brands or logos?

Our engagements are confidential. We do not publish client names, logos, products or supplier details.

How do you protect our interests?

Project confidentiality, defined responsibilities and written commercial agreements form part of our engagement process. NDAs are available before you share sensitive information.

Do you only work with products sourced through NGB?

No. We can build and advertise brands for products made anywhere, but we're strongest where we also know the supply chain.

Can you guarantee sales or ROAS?

No. Nobody honest can. We set targets, test, measure and report transparently on ROAS, CAC and conversion rate.

Do you manage ad spend?

We manage campaigns in your ad accounts. Media spend is paid by you directly to the platforms, and our fees are agreed separately.

How an enquiry works

  1. 01

    Share your requirement

    Describe the product, quantity, target price and destination market. Partial information is fine to start.

  2. 02

    Receive a feasibility assessment

    We assess whether, where and at what level India can make it, and flag compliance questions early.

  3. 03

    Review the proposed sourcing approach

    You receive a proposal covering manufacturing clusters, manufacturer types, sampling and timelines.

  4. 04

    Agree on the project scope

    Deliverables, responsibilities and fees are set out in a written agreement, with an NDA where needed.

  5. 05

    Begin supplier qualification and development

    We qualify manufacturers, manage samples and coordinate development towards an approved golden sample.

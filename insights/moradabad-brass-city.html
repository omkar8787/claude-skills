Moradabadthe brass city that sets tables around the world

How a city in Uttar Pradesh became India's best-known metalware cluster, the techniques its artisans use, and what buyers should know before ordering brass and metal products.

Peetal Nagri

Moradabad, in western Uttar Pradesh, is known across India as Peetal Nagri, the brass city. Generations of metalworkers have made it one of the country's best-known clusters for metal handicrafts, homeware and décor. Today it works in brass, but also in aluminium, iron, copper and stainless steel, for buyers in many countries.

How the work is done

A single piece can pass through several specialist workshops, often family-run, before it is finished. The cluster's strength is that network of skills.

  • Sand casting and lost-wax casting for solid forms
  • Spinning on a lathe for bowls, vases and lamp bases
  • Hand hammering, which leaves the facets that catch the light
  • Engraving and chasing (nakashi) for surface pattern
  • Plating, lacquering, antiquing and powder coating

From décor to barware

Moradabad makes everything from statement vases and lanterns to everyday tumblers, jiggers and serving trays. Barware and tableware have grown fast, and they bring an important question with them: is this item for food and drink?

What buyers should check

Metalware is straightforward to source when the specification is clear. Confirm these points with your supplier and your lab:

  • Food contact: raw brass and copper react with acidic food and drink, so check linings (tin, nickel or stainless) and test for migration in your market
  • Lead content in alloys and coatings, especially for the US (Proposition 65) and the EU
  • Finish durability: lacquers can scratch, and dishwasher claims need testing
  • Weight and wall thickness, which drive both price and feel
  • Packing for transit, since dents and scratches are the most common defects

The GI dimension

Moradabad Metal Craft is a registered Geographical Indication. A GI label can only be used by registered authorised users, so if a GI claim matters to your marketing, source from them. You can read the registration details on our Origin Collection page.

The sourcing lifecycle

  1. Output · Sourcing brief

    01

    Understand buyer requirements

    We turn your product, target price, quantity, market and compliance needs into a written brief that suppliers can quote against accurately.

    Output · Sourcing brief

  2. Output · Long-list

    02

    Identify suitable manufacturers

    We map the right clusters and manufacturer types, from artisan workshops to export factories, and approach a long-list.

    Output · Long-list

  3. Output · Comparison matrix

    03

    Evaluate supplier capabilities

    We verify registrations and certificates, compare quotations and MOQs, and assess capability. Where needed, we visit or audit.

    Output · Comparison matrix

  4. Output · Approved golden sample

    04

    Develop samples & prototypes

    Sampling rounds with structured feedback until a golden sample is approved and the specification is frozen.

    Output · Approved golden sample

  5. Output · Production tracker

    05

    Coordinate production

    Pre-production meeting, materials check and a production schedule, with regular status updates and early escalation of risks.

    Output · Production tracker

  6. Output · Inspection report

    06

    Conduct quality inspections

    Inline and AQL-based pre-shipment inspections against your specification, with photographic reports.

    Output · Inspection report

  7. Output · Shipping file

    07

    Export documentation & logistics

    Document checks, certificates of origin and handover to your forwarder, or one we coordinate.

    Output · Shipping file

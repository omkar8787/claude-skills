Three ways to make a product

OEM, ODM or private label?

OEM

Original Equipment Manufacturing

Manufacturing to your specifications. You own the design; the factory builds it to your drawings or tech pack.

Best when you have a defined design and need control of materials, tolerances and IP.

ODM

Original Design Manufacturing

Developing or customising a product from a manufacturer's existing or developed designs.

Best for speed: adapt a proven design with your changes, then refine it over time.

Private label

Your brand, made in India

Market-ready products sold under your own brand: labels, packaging and listings built for your channel.

Best for retailers, Amazon sellers and DTC brands launching or extending a range.

FAQ

Manufacturing: FAQs

What MOQs should I expect?

It depends on the product, materials and process. Some artisan products start at small quantities, while custom-dyed fabrics, moulds and printed packaging usually need larger runs. We confirm real MOQs with each manufacturer.

Who owns the design and tooling?

Agree it in writing before development. We recommend NDAs, and contracts that state ownership of designs, moulds and tooling.

Can you manage samples if I'm overseas?

Yes. We coordinate sample rounds, record feedback, and courier approved samples to you for sign-off.
